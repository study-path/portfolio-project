About

From spreadsheets to shipping software !

I am a web developer based in Ontario, Canada, who enjoys turning ideas into practical, user-friendly digital experiences. I build responsive applications and interfaces, working with Angular, Vue and React.

My background is in finance, working in bank back-office operations, managing foreign exchange and international payments, supporting Treasury operations, and making sure transactions, accounts, and reports reconciled correctly before the day was done. It taught me to think carefully about data, processes, and the people who use them. Today I work across both front-end development and business solutions on the Microsoft Power Platform, and I like projects where technology makes something simpler, clearer, or more useful.

When I am not at my computer 👩🏻‍💻 I like to stay active: snowboarding 🏂, wakeboarding 🌊, Pilates 🧘. I enjoy trying things that push me a little outside my comfort zone, and programming gives me the same feeling. I enjoy being a beginner, solving problems, and seeing myself improve.
